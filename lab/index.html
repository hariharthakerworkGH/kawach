Kawach design lab 2.1 not a production screen

Waiting for your decision

Three pieces are built and tested but are not in the app, because nothing has decided how each should behave. Choose, look at it, then press Confirm to lock it.

01 · Brand and identity

Who the product is before it says anything. One mark, one voice, one promise.

Your money, with clarity.

The same data. A calmer way to see it.
Name Kawach · shield

The app protects a person's future, so it is named for armour and it behaves like armour: quiet, always on, never asking to be admired.

02 · Atmosphere

Five coloured fields behind everything, at a strength you are not meant to notice.

The whole page is sitting on it right now. That is the test: if you had to be told, it is correct.

Cyan, upper left10%
Violet, upper right9%
Magenta, mid edge6%
Warm, lower right4%

The ceiling is measured, not chosen: above roughly a tenth of a wash at one pixel, the quietest text stops clearing 4.5:1 against its own background. The warm field is the weakest of the four and is kept to the lower right, well away from anything that warns, so a warning never has to compete with the wallpaper.

03 · Material hierarchy

Depth says what matters. Five levels, each a real step in fill, blur, line and shadow.

Answers: which of these things is the app telling me to look at?

L0 · flat

Solid, no blur. Structure and low-priority containers. Costs a mid-range phone nothing.

L1 · quiet

10px blur. A list, a row group, supporting information.

L2 · content

16px blur. A figure with something to say. The default.

L3 · focused

22px blur. What you opened, chose, or are being pointed at.

L4 · hero

30px blur. One per screen. The answer.

Depth communicates hierarchy. That is not the same as everything being glass, which is why the bottom of the scale is not glass at all.

04 · Editorial typography

A figure on its own is a readout. A figure with a sentence around it is a statement.

You are spending less.

₹19,960
this month ↓ 12.4% vs August

Groceries cost more than usual.

₹6,480
on food and dining ↑ ₹1,840

Three rules keep this from turning ornamental. The sentence is short enough to read in one go. The figure stays the largest thing on the surface. The tail carries the comparison rather than repeating the figure. Down is not automatically good, so the direction is set by whoever knows what the number means, never guessed from its sign.

06 · Controls

One geometry, three voices, and a target no smaller than a fingertip whatever the glyph looks like.

The toggle is 52 by 30 to look right and 44 tall to be pressed: an invisible strip carries the extra height without changing the layout. Every control here takes the same focus ring, and the ring is the only thing in the system allowed to draw a hard outline.

07 · Transaction rows

One line per payment. Everything else is behind a tap.

The mark on the left is the brand system, so a person finds a payment by recognising it rather than by reading it. The amount is the only thing allowed to be a money colour.

08 · Insight and context

The same fact, emphasised for the moment it is in. The arithmetic never changes; only the way it is said.

Answers: is this a normal day, or is something about to matter?

Everything looks steady
₹8,240 available

You are on track for this month.

Month end approaching
₹8,240 left for 6 days

That is ₹1,373 a day. You have used 71% of your budget.

Unusual spending
₹6,480 on food and dining

₹1,840 above your usual month.

All three say ₹8,240 or a figure taken from the same month. Nothing was recalculated to change the tone: emphasis is presentation, and it never reaches back into the number. Tone is set from a state the app can actually know - days remaining, a share of budget used, a category outside its own usual range - never invented to make a screen livelier.

09 · Charts

Four drawings. Each one is here because it answers a question, and the question is written above it.

Cash-flow river · six months

Is more coming in than going out, and how has that changed?

In · ₹52,000 Out · ₹43,760 The gap is what you kept

June is the month to look at: the lines almost touch, because ₹49,100 went out against ₹52,000 in. July is the widest gap, and it is the bonus. The scale is zoomed to the range the money actually moves in, so only the gap is filled - shading down to a baseline the axis does not start at is the lie this kind of chart usually tells.

Commitment meter

A quiet day keeps a visible stub rather than vanishing, so nothing reads as missing data.

Where it went

Where did the ₹19,960 go, biggest first?

This replaced the bubble constellation. A circle of circles is a handsome way to make a person squint at one small disc to work out whether it is bigger than another small disc; at 320px with fourteen categories it stops working entirely. A ranked bar answers the same question at a glance and keeps answering it as the list grows.

11 · Bottom sheets

A choice arrives from the bottom, covers what it must, and gives the screen back.

Category

Pinned inside a box here; in the app it is fixed to the window. It rises in 420ms on the one spring the system has, with an overshoot small enough that it reads as weight rather than as bounce.

12 · States

What the app says when it worked, when it did not, when there is nothing yet, and while it waits.

Payment saved
₹450 added to History.
Could not save
Nothing was lost. Try once more.
No payments yet
Add your first one, or paste a bank SMS.

Success and failure take one shape, because they are the same event with different news and a person should not have to learn two layouts to find out which one happened. An error says what survived before it says what broke.

13 · Navigation

Six places, always in the same order, with the current one lit rather than boxed.

The active item is brighter, heavier and glowing slightly. It is not given a filled pill, because a pill in a bar of six makes the other five look disabled.

14 · Motion

Three durations for the whole product. Motion explains a change; it never performs.

Fast · a press, a toggle140ms
Standard · a sheet, a transition240ms
Expressive · arriving420ms
Expressive ceiling · a chart600ms
A figure changing ₹12,400

Two numbers of the same size in the same place swap without being noticed. The small settle is what tells you it changed.

A payment arriving

Anything that would need longer than 600ms is staggered out of several of these, never stretched into one slow one. When the phone asks for less movement, every animation in the system stops and the state simply changes.

15 · Brand marks

Three steps, in order, so there is never a hole where a logo was not found.

The resolver is the app's own js/brand.js, running here rather than being described. No trademarked logo is drawn, approximated or stood in for with an emoji: a real mark appears only where a properly licensed file has been put in icons/brands/, and none is bundled today. The monogram takes its hue from the name itself, so a shop is the same colour on every screen forever.

16 · Responsive

A phone is the product. 320px is not an edge case, it is a person with an older handset.

At 320px wide
Left to spend
₹8,240
Month end approaching
₹8,240 left for 6 days

The hero figure is set in clamp(), so it shrinks with the viewport instead of breaking out of it: 68px on a 390 screen, 50px on a 320. Labels wrap, figures do not, and nothing on a row is allowed to push a money figure off the edge.

Daylight follows the phone rather than a switch in here. Set the system to light and this whole page becomes the light system: more opaque surfaces, borders doing the work the sheen does in the dark, and glow all but gone, because a lit edge on white reads as a smudge.

17 · Component catalogue

Every approved component, by name. Say the name and there is one thing it can mean.

Nothing here is a picture of a component. Each card renders the real one, from the same js/charts.js, css/dna.css and js/brand.js the app ships, so the catalogue cannot drift from what is on the screens.

18 · Brand library

Who Kawach would like a mark for, and what it currently has.

Every tile below is what the resolver draws today: no third-party mark is bundled, so each falls through to a category icon or a monogram. Nothing is drawn from memory and no logo is approximated - a wrong-looking mark is worse than a letter, and an unlicensed one is worse than both. Adding a real asset is one file plus one line in BRAND_ASSETS.
