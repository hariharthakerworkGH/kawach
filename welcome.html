Put it on your home screen

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install Kawach above, or Chrome's menu (three dots, top right) and then Install app.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Your data stays yours

  • Everything stays on your phone. There is no Kawach server, and nobody else can see your money.
  • Bank statements are read on the phone and never kept.
  • Backups are locked with a passphrase only you know.
  • No ads, no tracking, no bank logins.

Works best with

Statement PDFs from any Indian bank or credit card, and CSV downloads. Bank SMS alerts can be shared straight into the app on Android.
